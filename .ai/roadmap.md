# Product Roadmap v1.0

---

# PURPOSE

This roadmap defines the planned evolution of Hire Huub ERP.

It provides a clear vision of future modules, milestones and release goals.

This document is the primary planning reference for all future development.

Roadmap changes require Product Owner approval.

---

# PRODUCT VISION

Hire Huub ERP is a modern Human Resource Management System (HRMS) designed for staffing and recruitment organizations.

Mission

Deliver a secure, scalable and AI-powered HR platform that simplifies recruitment, employee management, payroll, attendance and workforce operations.

Company

Hire Huub

Tagline

The Right People. The Right Job.
